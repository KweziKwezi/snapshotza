# SnapshotZA: fees and refunds (proposal for you to edit)

Status: **DRAFT for the owner to edit.** Nothing in the app has been changed to match this yet. Once you are happy with
it, it becomes (1) the rules built into the app and (2) the plain-language Terms and Refund Policy, which a South
African attorney should check before you take real money.

How to use this: read it, change anything marked **[EDIT]**, answer the **questions in section 9**, and send it back
(or tell me the changes in chat). Where I wrote a number, it is a suggestion, not a fact.

## 1. The short version

| | Proposal |
|---|---|
| Client pays | The package price, **100%**, upfront. No extra fees on top. |
| Our commission | **7% of the package price** (paid by the photographer, out of their share). |
| Payment processing | The gateway's cost, shown to the photographer as **3.5% of the package price** (about what PayFast charges for cards: 3.5% + R2 [EDIT: confirm with the gateway you choose]). |
| Photographer receives | Price minus commission minus processing. Called the **net amount**. |
| Paid in two parts | **Deposit** = 30% / 40% / 50% of the net (Tier 1 / 2 / 3), released when the shoot is confirmed. **Final** = the rest, released when the client approves the photos (or after 48 hours). |
| Deposit refundable? | **No.** Once released, the deposit is the photographer's. |
| Client cancels | **Standard: no refund, but the date can be moved** (photographer approves). **With Refund Protection** (optional, **R50 non-refundable** at checkout): full refund if cancelled **at least 48 hours before the shoot**. Under 48 hours: no refund for anyone. |
| Photographer no-show | Client gets **everything back, 100%**, within **14 days**. We carry the fees. |
| Dispute: client wins | Client gets the **final part** back. Deposit stays with the photographer. |
| Dispute: photographer wins | Photographer gets the final part. |

"Fees on both payments": every payout statement shows its own share of the commission and processing fee, so the
deposit and the final payment each carry their fair share (see the worked example).

## 3. Refunds: what happens in each situation

All refunds go back to the **original payment method**, and are paid **within 14 days** of the decision. (The law,
section 44 of the Electronic Communications and Transactions Act, allows up to 30 days; we promise faster.)

Prices below use the R2,400 / Tier 1 example. "Platform" means SnapshotZA.

### 3a. The client cancels before the shoot (Refund Protection)

At checkout the client chooses one of two options. Both are explained on screen before paying, with a tick box.

| Option | What the client pays | If they cancel |
|---|---|---|
| **Standard** | The package price (R2,400) | **No refund.** They can move the date instead (3e). |
| **Refund Protection** (optional) | The package price **plus R50** (the R50 is never refunded) | **Full refund of the package price** if they cancel **at least 48 hours before the shoot starts**. Less than 48 hours before: no refund. |

R2,400 / Tier 1 example. "Platform" means SnapshotZA.

| Situation | Client gets back | Photographer gets | Platform |
|---|---|---|---|
| Standard, cancels at any time | R0 | **R2,148** (full net), paid after the original shoot date passes | keeps R168 commission |
| Protection, cancels **48h or more** before | **R2,400** (see the fee options below) | R0 (the date is released so they can re-sell it) | keeps the R50; the R84 payment fee we already paid is lost |
| Protection, cancels **under 48h** before | R0 | **R2,148** | keeps R168 commission and the R50 |
| **Cooling-off** (legal minimum, see warning below): within 7 days of booking, shoot more than 7 days away | **R2,400** | R0 | absorbs the R84 payment fee |

Refunds go back to the original payment method within **14 days** of the cancellation.

**Warning: this rule meets two laws.** (1) The Electronic Communications and Transactions Act (ECTA) section 44
gives online buyers a 7-day cooling-off right that the business cannot sign away. If it applies to a shoot booked for a
specific date, a client who books and changes their mind within 7 days must be refunded even without Refund Protection.
That is why the cooling-off row is there; **remove it only if your attorney confirms it does not apply.** (2) The
Consumer Protection Act section 17 lets a client cancel an advance booking and lets you charge only a *reasonable*
cancellation charge. "No refund at all unless you bought protection" may be challenged, which is why moving the date
is always allowed for free.

**Does R50 cover your cost? [EDIT: pick one]** A refund costs you the payment fee you already paid (the gateway does
not give it back) plus the cost of sending the refund.

| Package price | Payment fee lost (3.5%) | R50 kept minus that fee |
|---|---|---|
| R1,200 | R42 | **+R8** |
| R2,400 | R84 | **-R34** |
| R4,000 | R140 | **-R90** |

(Before the small bank fee for sending the refund.) So a flat R50 only covers small packages. Options:

- **A. Flat R50, full price refunded.** Simplest to explain ("get all your money back"). You lose money on bigger packages.
- **B. Flat R50, refund = price minus the payment fee.** You never lose, but the client sees R2,316 back on a R2,400 booking, which feels like a trick if not shown upfront.
- **C. (my suggestion) Protection price by package size, full price refunded.** For example R50 for packages up to R1,500, R100 up to R3,000, R150 above. Easy to explain, and it covers the payment fee and the refund transfer.

### 3b. The photographer cancels, or is a no-show

| Situation | Client gets back | Photographer | Platform |
|---|---|---|---|
| **Photographer cancels** at any time | **R2,400 (100%)** | R0, plus a strike | absorbs the R84 processing fee, keeps no commission |
| **Photographer no-show** (reported by the client and confirmed by our team) | **R2,400 (100%)**, within 14 days | R0, plus a strike. [EDIT: suspend on the first no-show?] | absorbs the R84 fee, keeps no commission |

This matches your rule: full refund, and the fee is given back to the client too (so the client gets back exactly
what they paid). The cost of the gateway fee is ours.

[EDIT] Strikes: 1st = warning and visible strike, 2nd = 30-day suspension, 3rd = removed from the platform.

### 3c. The client does not show up (client no-show)

The photographer held the date. **No refund.** The photographer gets the full net (R2,148) as if the shoot
happened, minus nothing extra. [EDIT: alternatively, 50% back to the client if they contact us within 24 hours.]

### 3d. A problem after the shoot (a "dispute")

Our team looks at the booking (messages, photos, what each side says) and decides within **5 working days**
[EDIT]. The deposit has already been released to the photographer, so it stays with them.

| Decision | Client gets back | Photographer gets | Platform |
|---|---|---|---|
| **Client wins** (for example, photos never delivered, or not what was agreed) | **R1,680**: the whole final part (70% of the price) | keeps the R644.40 deposit; the final part is not paid | keeps R50.40 commission on the deposit part; loses R58.80 processing on the refunded part |
| **Photographer wins** | R0 | **R1,503.60** final part, on top of the deposit | keeps its full R168 commission |
| **Split decision** [EDIT: do you want this option?] | agreed amount, e.g. 50% of the final part | the rest | proportional |

The refund formula in plain words: **the client gets back the share of the booking that has not been paid out yet**
(for Tier 1: 70% of the price; Tier 2: 60%; Tier 3: 50%). Our commission on that refunded part is also returned to
the client.

### 3e. Changing the date (the only flexibility on Standard bookings)

- The client asks for a new date and time in the app. The photographer **approves or declines**.
- **Free**, if asked **at least 48 hours** before the shoot [EDIT], and the new time is free in the photographer's diary.
- If the photographer declines (or does not answer within 48 hours [EDIT: the request lapses, the client is told]),
  the booking stays on the original date. The client can ask again with different dates.
- Maximum of **2 date changes** per booking [EDIT].
- A date change does not change the price, the deposit rules or the 48-hour refund cut-off (the cut-off moves with the
  new date).
- If the photographer later cancels, it is a photographer cancellation (3b): the client gets everything back.

### 3f. Photos are delivered but the client goes quiet

If the client does not respond within **48 hours**, the photos count as approved and the final payment is released
(already how the app works). Full-size downloads stay available for **15 days**, then are deleted.

## 4. When the photographer gets paid (timeline)

1. Client pays: money is held by us (not yet the photographer's).
2. Shoot happens. Photographer taps **Mark shoot complete**. Client taps **Yes, my shoot happened**, or 24 hours
   pass and it confirms by itself. **Deposit released.**
3. Photographer uploads all the edited photos and taps **Deliver**.
4. Client approves, or 48 hours pass. **Final payment released.**
5. Photographer asks to withdraw; our team sends the transfer and marks it sent.
6. If anyone reports a problem, any money **not yet withdrawn** is frozen until our team decides.

[EDIT] Minimum withdrawal: R100. Withdrawals paid within **2 working days**.

## 5. Protection for photographers

- A client cannot get the deposit back (once released).
- A client cannot dispute after the photos are approved, or after **7 days** past the 48-hour window [EDIT].
- Frivolous or false reports (for example, claiming a no-show when the photographer arrived) can lead to the
  client's account being warned or closed.
- Card chargebacks: if a client disputes a card payment with their bank after we have paid you, the bank can
  take the money back from us. [EDIT] Suggested rule: a photographer must repay a chargeback caused by their own
  failure to deliver, and we hold the final payment for the review window (already in place) to reduce the risk.

## 6. Protection for clients

- Money is held until the shoot is confirmed; the final part until the photos are delivered.
- Full refund (including fees) if the photographer cancels or does not show up.
- Photos available for 15 days. Chats are deleted after 20 days (kept longer only while a problem report is open).
- A clear way to report a problem, with a human decision and a written reason.

## 8. Things a lawyer must check (I am not a lawyer; this is not legal advice)

- **Consumer Protection Act (section 17):** advance bookings may be cancelled by the consumer, subject to a
  *reasonable* charge. Is a 0% refund inside 48 hours "reasonable" for a photography booking? Is the 50% band safe?
- **ECTA (section 44):** 7-day cooling-off for online purchases, refund within 30 days. Whether it applies to a
  photo shoot on a specific date is a point to confirm (some services booked for a specific date are excluded).
- **Refund Protection as a product:** an optional paid add-on that unlocks refunds. Check it is not treated as
  insurance, and that the price and the 48-hour rule are shown clearly before payment.
- **Clear disclosure:** the cancellation ladder and the non-refundable deposit must be shown **before** the client
  pays, and accepted with a tick box (the app will do this).
- **POPIA:** privacy policy, retention periods (photos 15 days, chats 20 days), a person responsible for personal
  information, what happens to accounts that are closed.
- **Marketplace position:** you are an intermediary. The agreement for the shoot is between client and
  photographer; you hold the money and run the rules. The Terms must say this clearly.
- **Payment holding:** holding client money for photographers can count as a regulated activity in some setups.
  Check whether you need a payment provider that holds funds on your behalf (a "split payments" or escrow product)
  rather than you holding it in your own account.
